There are days when I still surprise myself by being alive.

I grew up with a body many thought too fragile, and I learned that strength isn't measured in muscle, but in the stubbornness of getting up one more time. If they've told you that you don't belong where you are, don't believe them: no one can measure your will from the outside.

I learned to tell courage from recklessness: courage is looking at the abyss and taking one step even when your whole body trembles; recklessness is jumping without looking and calling it destiny. I also learned that we are not our surnames: the moment comes when you decide which part of that inheritance you keep and which you burn.

Fire doesn't only destroy; it also cleanses. Don't let anyone write your story for you. Not even me.

— Violet

P. S.: your body is not your enemy. It brought you here, and that already counts.

Human. Don't take offense: time puts every name in its place, as it puts the sun in the sky.

Listen, because I don't repeat myself. The bond is not a prize: it is a chain forged in fire, and fire does not distinguish between the one who lights it and the one who burns. When you decide to fly with me, your life no longer belongs entirely to you. Don't ask me for tenderness; I was not carved for it. But when you sleep, I lower my head, and when you cry, I stay near without you noticing. Call it habit.

The sky does not forgive doubt. Up there there is only room for what is essential: air, motion, will. Leave everything heavy below; I know every current and every invisible trap. You will trust, you will doubt, and still you will not fall, because I would not allow it.

Don't seek my approval: there is none. Be worthy of the sky and the sky will answer. Fly.

— Tairn

P. S.: feed yourself well. I don't mean me. I mean you.

There are things you don't say out loud in the quadrant. That's why I'm writing them down.

I'm not the strongest or the fastest. I knew it from day one and, instead of hiding, I made lists: who watches the flank, who hasn't slept, who carries extra water. That's my way of fighting, and it has kept my people alive more than once. I know of no more honest victory.

Don't measure your courage by what you endure alone: measure it by what you hold up with others. A shield isn't a wall; it's an agreement. I raise mine and you raise yours, and between the two there's a space where you can breathe and trust. Speak clearly, even with a shaking voice: the first time I said no, the world didn't end. If it's ever your turn to lead, people follow those who truly see them.

— Rhiannon

P. S.: always carry extra water. Someone will thank you.

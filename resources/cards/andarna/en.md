Hello, hello! Surprised that I'm speaking first? The small ones have things to say too.

I'm young and I'm not ashamed of it: everything amazes me, storms and new colors. Nobody told me which color to choose, so I chose my own. It cost me sideways glances, but I learned something: your own place isn't found, it's built. Stone by stone, wingbeat by wingbeat. If you wait for someone to tell you where you fit, you'll wait seated your whole life.

I confess I get scared too: of long nights, of not being enough. But fear isn't your enemy; it's your travel companion. Take its hand and keep going.

Play more, seriously. Every prank is a lesson in disguise and every laugh a shield. And take care of your own even if you're small: there's no size requirement for courage.

— Andarna

P. S.: if you ever see me running toward something and not away from it, I'll know you learned.

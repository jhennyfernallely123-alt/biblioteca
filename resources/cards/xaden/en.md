Don't expect me to ask for your trust: trust isn't asked for; it's proven.

I grew up learning that every word costs dearly if you hand it to the wrong person. That's why I keep my names in secrets and my plans in shadows. I don't love the dark; it's just that light betrays whoever moves through it. If you're reading this, someone decided you deserved to know. Think before you thank them: information leaves marks.

There are people worth burning for. When you find yours, don't let go: protect them even with trembling hands, even if lying is the price. Loyalty isn't a pretty feeling; it's a decision you repeat every morning. Distrust anyone who offers safety in exchange for your secrets: ask what the other person gains from your silence.

The rest, you'll learn along the way. We all learn, though none of us asks to.
